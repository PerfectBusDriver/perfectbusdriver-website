GPS Tracker

Record and follow your circuits in real-time. Multilingual voice guidance, deviation detection and automatic route recalculation.

Detailed Statistics

Distance traveled, number of stops. Export/Share your circuits in KML, GPX, JSON or CSV for analysis.

Time Management

Amplitude calculators, weekly and daily rest alerts compliant with regulations, break management.

Points of Interest

Complete Documentation

Oral exam sheets, RSE regulatory documentation, European speed limits. Everything at hand.

Professional Tools

Specialized calculators, converters, passenger counter, personal document management, driver planning, FIMO/FCO information. A complete toolbox.
