2 Your routes, exactly the same

The circuit tracker

✕ It's not a sat nav

A sat nav picks the route for you. The tracker makes you follow your route: the one you recorded, with your stops, in order. Perfect for a school run, a regular line or a pick-up round you don't know yet.

  1. ⏺️

    Record

    Tap Start, mark each stop with one tap, then Finish. The track keeps recording even with the screen off.

  2. 💾

    Save and share

    Name your circuit, find it in “My circuits” and send it to a colleague.

  3. 🧭

    Drive it again

    The app guides you along your track, announces the next stop and warns you if you stray from it.
